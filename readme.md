# Bogor Kasohor

> **"A BEAUTIFUL, RELAXED, AND COMFORTABLE CITY, LIKE A FLOWER IN A GARDEN"**

A simple and informative website created to introduce the history, legendary culinary delights, and proud icons of Bogor City.

## 📑 Table of Contents (Main Features)

This website consists of several main sections accessible via the navigation menu:

* **History (*Buitenzorg*):** Briefly explains the history of Bogor City, starting from the roots of the Sunda Kingdom of Pakuan Pajajaran, the naming of *Buitenzorg* by Governor-General Gustaaf Willem Baron van Imhoff, to the establishment of the Bogor Botanical Gardens (*s’Lands Plantentuin*) in 1817.
* **Legendary Culinary Tourism:**
    A list of highly recommended traditional Bogor foods to try, along with descriptions and illustrations:
    * 🍲 **Soto Mie Bogor:** A mix of yellow noodles, rice vermicelli, beef, and rissole in a fresh, savory broth.
    * 🥗 **Toge Goreng Bogor:** Boiled bean sprouts topped with a savory *tauco* (fermented soy paste) and *oncom* sauce, usually served with *ketupat* (rice cakes).
    * 🥣 **Asinan Bogor:** Fresh vegetables or fruits served with a refreshing vinegar broth and sprinkled with peanuts.
* **City Gallery:**
    A collection of iconic portraits of Bogor City, featuring Tugu Kujang (Kujang Monument), Kebun Raya Bogor (Bogor Botanical Gardens), and the Biskita public transportation.

## 🛠️ Website Functionalities

* **Responsive Navigation:** Equipped with a hamburger menu for an optimal viewing experience on smaller screens or mobile devices.
* **Smooth Scroll Navigation:** Features anchor links that direct users straight to specific sections within the page seamlessly.
* **Back to Top Button:** An interactive button that appears when scrolling down, making it easier for users to instantly return to the top of the page.
